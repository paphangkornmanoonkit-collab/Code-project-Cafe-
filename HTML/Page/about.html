Our Story

How a small dream in the Sing Buri countryside grew into a peaceful garden retreat.

2020

The Seed of an Idea

Koh Sawan started as a small vision — creating a garden sanctuary where travelers could slow down and rest among the palms.

2022

Doors Open in Sing Buri

We officially opened our cafe, bringing sandy beach-like floors and a tropical island feel to the heart of the countryside.

2024

Growing the Experience

We expanded our food menu with light savory dishes and built our photo booth corner for guests to capture special moments.

2026

Welcome to Koh Sawan

Today, we continue serving honestly brewed coffee and fresh treats to everyone looking to relax and enjoy the moment.
